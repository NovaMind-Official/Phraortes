Tools

Everything it can build for you.

Fourteen kinds of live tools appear inside an answer. Each one below is the real thing: change a number, drag, rotate, run it.

01

01 · Solve

Every step, in order.

Ask a problem and get the worked solution one step at a time, with the reasoning and not only the answer.

  • Math
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
Try asking
  • A 2 kg block slides down a 30° ramp. Find the acceleration.
  • Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0 step by step

You get numbered steps with typeset formulas and a clear final answer, not a wall of text.

A 2 kg block slides down a frictionless 30° ramp. What is its acceleration?

02 · Graph

See the function, not the formula.

Pan, pinch and tap to read values. Roots and intersections are found for you.

Try asking
  • Plot sin(x) and x²/4
  • Where does ln(x) cross x − 2?

Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a curve to read exact values. Roots and intersections are marked for you.

Plot sin(x) and x²/4 and show where they meet.

03 · Molecules

Chemistry you can turn around.

Rotate molecules in 3D, then balance any equation with a tap.

Try asking
  • Show me caffeine in 3D
  • Balance Fe + O2 → Fe2O3

Rotate with one finger. The balancer shows each coefficient and checks atoms on both sides.

Show me water, methane and benzene in 3D.

Electron configurations, shells and a Bohr model for every element.

Try asking
  • Electron configuration of iron
  • Compare sodium and chlorine

Every element has its shells, a Bohr model and its full configuration.

Where is gold on the periodic table? Show its electron configuration.

05 · Simulate

Change a number. Watch it happen.

Charge a capacitor, launch a projectile, swing a pendulum. Every slider is live.

Try asking
  • Simulate a 45° projectile at 20 m/s
  • Charge a 100 µF capacitor through 10 kΩ

Sliders change the model live. Graphs and values update as you drag.

Simulate charging a 200 µF capacitor through 5 kΩ from 9 V.

06 · Code

Describe it. Run it.

Ask for a small animation, game or calculator and it runs right inside the chat. Edit the code, run it again and read the console.

Try asking
  • Build a bouncing ball animation
  • Make a BMI calculator
  • Draw a clock with canvas

Runs in a locked sandbox with no network and no access to your chats. Tabs show Preview, HTML, JS and Console.

Build a bouncing ball animation.

07 · Statistics

Paste numbers. Get the picture.

Mean, median, spread, histogram, box plot and regression, computed on the spot. Edit the data and everything updates.

Try asking
  • Mean and standard deviation of 12, 15, 9, 20, 17
  • Is there a correlation between hours studied and score?

Paired data adds a scatter plot, a regression line, r and R², and a predictor you can slide.

Analyse 12, 15, 9, 20, 17, 11, 14 and show the spread.

Any question that needs a look-up shows its steps as they happen.

09 · Look-ups

It looks things up, so you don't have to.

Phraortes can use tools in the middle of an answer and shows every step it took.

A

Web search

Finds current information instead of guessing from old memory.“Who won the latest Nobel Prize in Physics?”Searches, reads the results and answers with the source.

B

Read any page

Give it a link and ask about what is inside.“Summarize https://example.com/article in 5 points”Opens the page and answers from what is actually on it.

C

Weather & prices

Live weather and crypto prices in one line.“Weather in Tokyo, and the Bitcoin price”Two live look-ups, one short answer.

D

Files

Each chat has its own workspace for your files.“Put these notes in a file called plan.md”Each chat keeps its own files, ready to download.

E

Voice

Images
