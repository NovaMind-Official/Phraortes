Press kit

Phraortes, in one page.

Real screenshots, facts and brand colours for posters, video and articles. Everything here is accurate and free to use when presenting Phraortes.

What it is

  • Phraortes is an AI assistant that builds the answer: it acts, not only replies.
  • Works in the browser and installs like an app on iPhone, iPad and Android. Free to start, nothing to install.
  • Built entirely from a phone.
  • Website: novamind-official.github.io/Phraortes/ · App: novamind-official.github.io/
  • Languages: English, Persian, German, Russian, Chinese and Turkish (website).

Headline features

  • Minds — build an assistant with a job and share it as one link.
  • Rooms — private encrypted group chat; call the AI with @ai.
  • Agent — voice, real timers and alarms, a voice whiteboard, confirm cards with Undo.
  • Council — four opposing minds, then a judge.
  • Professional code — highlighting, line numbers, full-screen editor.
  • Save as file — Word, Excel, PDF or Markdown in one tap.

Coming soon: API access for Pro and Max. Please do not present it as available.
