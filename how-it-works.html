How it works

From a question to understanding.

Phraortes is built around one idea: you learn more from something you can see and change than from a paragraph.

Step 1

Ask in your own words.

No special syntax and no prompt tricks. Write the problem the way you would say it to a teacher, in English or Persian. You can also send a photo of a problem or just talk.

Explore, then practice.

The answer arrives with the graph, molecule, circuit or simulation built in. Change a number and see what happens. Then ask it to quiz you, so you find out what you really understood.

Privacy

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan gives you every study tool with a limited number of messages. Paid plans lift the limit and unlock stronger cores.

Which subjects does it cover?

Math, physics and chemistry are the focus: graphs, simulations, step-by-step solutions, molecules, the periodic table and circuits. You can ask about other topics too.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Ask in English or Persian and the solutions, formulas and tools follow your language.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" to open it like an app.

Can I trust the answers?

Phraortes can make mistakes. Check important results, especially numbers, against a textbook or your teacher.
