How it works

From a question to done.

Phraortes is built around one idea: a good answer is something you can see, change and act on, not a paragraph.

Ask in your own words.

Type it, snap a photo, upload a PDF, talk to Aura, or just say “Hey Phraortes”. No special syntax. English or Persian.

Watch it work.

A short live summary shows each step as it happens: searching, reading, calculating. When it is done, the steps fold into one line you can reopen.

Let it act.

Ask for an alarm and a real one is set. Ask for a drawing and the whiteboard opens. Ask for a big goal and it plans the steps and runs them. This is the agent.

Explore, then practice.

The answer arrives with the graph, molecule, circuit, dashboard or app built in. Change a number and see what happens. Then ask for a quiz to find out what stuck.

Under the hood

What is going on behind the answer.

◆
A

You choose the core

Four AI cores, from fast and light to the most powerful. All are open on every plan. Start on Prime and move up when the problem gets harder.

⚙
B

Tools run on demand

When a question needs it, Phraortes searches the web, reads a page, checks a price, sets a timer or draws, then tells you what it did.

⬡
C

Your chats stay with you

Conversations are stored in your browser. Code you run stays in a sandbox with no network access.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Every plan includes every tool and all four cores. Paid plans raise the limits, and Max removes them.

What can I ask it?

Anything. It is an everything-AI: writing, research, code, data, maths and science, planning, and getting things done on your phone.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Ask in English or Persian and answers, formulas, commands and the wake word follow your language.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in the browser and installs like an app from Safari.

What does the agent need from me?

Nothing beyond a tap where iOS requires one: to start the microphone, to open another app or to place a call.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser, on your device.

Ask it something.

Free to start. Nothing to install.
