Features

Everything in the box, with an example.

Every feature below comes with something you can type right now and what you get back.

Try it freeSee pricing
01

Step-by-step solutions

Ask a math, physics or chemistry problem and get a worked solution: what is given, what to find, each step with its formula, and a clear final answer. Every formula is typeset, so it reads like a textbook.

Try asking
  • A 2 kg block slides down a frictionless 30° ramp. What is its acceleration?

You get: numbered steps with typeset formulas, ending in a = 4.9 m/s².

See it live

02

Live steps

While it works, a short live summary says what is happening: reading your question, searching the web, checking a number, writing the answer. When it finishes, the steps fold into one line you can open again.

Try asking
  • What is the population of Iceland today?

You get: “Searching the web… Reading statistics.is… Writing the answer”, then “Thought for 6s · 3 steps”.

See it live

03

Interactive graphs

Plot one or several functions. Pan, pinch and tap to read exact values. Roots and intersections are found and marked for you.

Try asking
  • Plot sin(x) and x²/4 and show where they meet.

You get: a graph you can drag and zoom, with the intersection points labelled.

See it live

04

3D molecules and equation balancer

Show any common molecule in 3D and turn it with one finger. Give it an unbalanced equation and it finds the coefficients and checks the atoms on both sides.

Try asking
  • Show me caffeine in 3D. Then balance Fe + O2 → Fe2O3.

You get: a rotatable 3D model and a balanced equation: 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃.

See it live

05

Periodic table

All 118 elements, each with its shells, a Bohr model and its full electron configuration.

Try asking
  • Where is gold on the periodic table? Show its electron configuration.

You get: the table with gold highlighted, plus its Bohr model and configuration.

See it live

06

Simulations and circuits

Charge a capacitor, launch a projectile, swing a pendulum, build a circuit. Every slider is live and the graphs update as you drag.

Try asking
  • Simulate charging a 200 µF capacitor through 5 kΩ from 9 V.

You get: a live RC simulation with the voltage curve and the time constant.

See it live

07

Code playground

Ask for a small animation, game or calculator and it runs right inside the chat. Edit the code, run it again and read the console. It runs in a locked sandbox with no network access and cannot see your chats.

Try asking
  • Build a bouncing ball animation.

You get: a working animation, with tabs for Preview, HTML, JS and Console.

See it live

08

Statistics lab

Paste numbers and get mean, median, quartiles, standard deviation, a histogram and a box plot. Give it paired data and it adds a scatter plot, a regression line, r and R² and a predictor.

Try asking
  • Mean and standard deviation of 12, 15, 9, 20, 17.

You get: a summary table, a histogram and a box plot you can edit live.

See it live

09

Web search, pages, weather and prices

Phraortes can search the web, read a page you link, check the weather and look up crypto prices in the middle of an answer, and it shows every step it took. Each chat also has its own file workspace.

Try asking
  • Summarize https://example.com/article in 5 points.

You get: a short summary taken from the page itself.

See it live

10

Quizzes and flashcards

Turn what you just learned into practice. It writes multiple-choice questions with explanations and flashcards you can flip.

Try asking
  • Quiz me on molar mass.

You get: a quiz with instant feedback, or a deck of flip cards.

See it live

11

Voice and images

Talk to Aura and hear the answer back, available on the Shahanshah plan. Describe a picture and get an image right in the chat.

Try asking
  • An image of a cell dividing, textbook style.

You get: the image, inside the conversation.

12

Private by design

Your chats are stored in your browser, on your device, not on our servers. Code you run stays in a sandbox with no network access.

Try asking
  • Close the tab and come back.

You get: your conversations, exactly where you left them.

13

Installs like an app

Open it in the browser, then add it to your home screen. No store, no download, and it works full screen like an app.

Try asking
  • Add to Home Screen from your browser’s share menu.

You get: an app icon and a full-screen Phraortes.

14

Photo solve

Photograph a problem from your notebook or a textbook and Phraortes reads it, solves it and explains every step. It works on printed and handwritten questions.

Try asking
  • Send a photo of a physics homework question.

You get: the problem read correctly, then a worked solution you can follow.

15

Exam mode

Pick a subject and a level and take a timed practice test. At the end you get a score, a breakdown by topic and a tutor button that explains the questions you missed.

Try asking
  • Start a 10-question chemistry exam.

You get: a scored test with a per-topic breakdown and explanations.

16

Probability lab

Dice, coins, a Galton board and a Monte Carlo estimate of π. Roll thousands of trials in one tap and watch the real results settle onto the theory.

Try asking
  • Roll two dice 1000 times and show the distribution.

You get: a live histogram with the theoretical curve over it.

See it live

17

Optics

Converging and diverging lenses, concave and convex mirrors. Drag the object and the principal rays, the image and the magnification update live.

Try asking
  • Where does the image form for an object 25 cm from a 10 cm lens?

You get: a ray diagram with the image, its type and the magnification.

See it live

18

Sources you can open

When an answer comes from a real web search, a small “sources” pill appears under it. Tap it to see every search that was run, every page that was found or read, and a short excerpt of each. Filter by search, open any page, or copy all the links. Nothing is added from memory.

Try asking
  • What is the Bitcoin price today?

You get: an answer with a sources pill that opens the pages behind it.

19

Dark, light and your own accent

Switch between dark and light, then pick an accent color: gold, emerald, sky, violet, rose or orange. Or leave it on Auto and the color follows the mood of your conversation.

Try asking
  • Open Account, then Settings, then Accent color.

You get: the whole app in the colors you chose, remembered next time.

20

Actions under every answer

A small ··· menu under each answer lets you listen to it, focus on it, share it as an image or give feedback. Copy and regenerate are one tap away.

Try asking
  • Tap ··· under any answer.

You get: a native-style menu with listen, focus, share and feedback.

21

Digital logic lab

Type a Boolean expression and get the gate circuit with live switches, an output LED, the full truth table and the minterms. Tap a row and the inputs jump to it. Good for logic gates, Boolean algebra and computer-architecture courses.

Try asking
  • Draw (A XOR B) NAND (C OR NOT D) and show the truth table.

You get: a gate diagram you can switch on and off, plus the truth table.

See it live

22

Read aloud

Listen to any answer in the best voice installed on your phone. It reads sentence by sentence, skips code, says formulas in words, and you can change the speed and the voice.

Try asking
  • Tap ··· under an answer, then Listen.

You get: a small player with pause, speed and voice controls.

23

CPU simulator

Write a few lines of assembly and step through them on a tiny 8-bit CPU. Registers, flags and memory update after every instruction, and a loop or a jump is easy to follow. Good for computer architecture and low-level programming courses.

Try asking
  • Write an assembly loop that adds 1 to 5 and run it on the CPU.

You get: a program you can step through, with the registers and memory changing live.

See it live

24

Breadboard and LEDs

An LED, a resistor and a supply on a breadboard. Change the voltage and the resistor and see the current, the resistor colour bands and a warning before the LED burns out.

Try asking
  • Which resistor do I need for a red LED on 9 V?

You get: a breadboard you can adjust, with the current and the right resistor value.

See it live

25

Deep Research

Ask a big question and Phraortes runs several real web searches at once, opens the best pages, and writes a structured report with its sources. Pick Quick for three searches or Deep for six searches and up to six pages.

Try asking
  • Open Tools, tap Deep Research and ask: what is the state of solid-state batteries?

You get: a sourced report, with every search and page it used one tap away.

26

Siri and Shortcuts

Build a link that opens Phraortes with your question already typed, or sends it for you. Add it to the Shortcuts app and say “Hey Siri” followed by its name. Works for plain questions and for Deep Research.

Try asking
  • Open Tools, tap Siri Links, type a question and copy the link.

You get: a link you can put on your Home Screen or run by voice.

27

Daily Challenge and Lab Report

Three fresh questions a day with a streak that rewards showing up, and a lab-report builder that turns your measurements into a clean write-up with a table, a result and an error discussion.

Try asking
  • Open Tools, then Daily Challenge.

You get: three questions with instant explanations, and a streak that grows.

28

Compare answers

When a question has more than one good answer, Phraortes offers a second take below the first. Open it to see both side by side and keep the one you prefer. A second take uses one message.

Try asking
  • Ask for two different ways to learn the quadratic formula.

You get: two answers side by side, with the differences marked.

29

Try asking
  • Derive the range of a projectile on a slope.

You get: deeper reasoning when you choose a stronger core.
