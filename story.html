Our story

Built entirely from a phone.

No laptop. No office. One person, one screen, and a stubborn idea: studying should feel like touching the thing you are learning.

Why it exists

Answers were never the problem.

Every AI can give you an answer. Very few help you see why it is true. A graph you can drag, a molecule you can turn, a circuit you can break: that is when a formula finally clicks.

Phraortes started from that one frustration. Explain the reasoning first, then build the thing inside the answer so you can poke at it.

One phone. Every line.

The whole product, the app, the tools, the backend and this website, was written and tested on a phone. That limit shaped the design: everything runs in the browser, nothing needs installing, and it stays light enough to open anywhere.

What we will not compromise

  • Private by default. Chats stay in your browser.
  • Honest. AI can make mistakes, so it shows its work and you can check it.
  • Free to start. No card, no install.

Try it. Then tell us what is missing.

Every feature here began as someone saying “can it also…”.
