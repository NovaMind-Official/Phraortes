What is new.

Newest first. We ship small and often.

Just shipped
Wake word“Hey Phraortes” hands-free mode: tap once, then talk. It answers out loud. Works while the app is open and on screen.
WhiteboardA voice whiteboard: “line, then a circle under it, then a cube” and each shape is drawn live, in order. 25+ shapes, colours, undo and save. Draw with your finger too.
AgentThe agent now really acts: real timers, alarms and reminders, plus one-tap WhatsApp, Telegram, call, mail and maps.
SiteA new Agent page and a rebuilt Features, How it works, Compare and Changelog.
PositioningPhraortes is an AI for everything, not only study.
Newest
AgentSay “remind me at 5pm” or “تایمر ۱۰ دقیقه” and a real timer, alarm or reminder is set. It rings with sound and an alert. Add it to Calendar and it rings even when the app is closed. It also prepares one-tap buttons for WhatsApp, Telegram, calls, messages, mail and maps.
CoresEvery core is now open on every plan. Daily limits on the heavy cores grow with the plan, and Max has none. Free messages went from 10 to 25 per window.
LabA virtual chemistry lab: a foam volcano, magnesium and acid, elephant toothpaste, titration and an alkali metal. Add the reagents and watch them react.
ResearchDeep research ends with an evidence board that links every claim to its sources and flags the weak ones.
ReviewSpaced-repetition cards that come back right before you forget, and knowledge maps you can mark as known or unsure.
FilesUpload PDFs and several images at once. Scanned PDFs are read as page images.
SiteA new Pricing page with four 3D plan medals you can turn, and a Learn page where a knowledge map fills in as you scroll. The home page ring now holds 12 tools.
CoachQuestion cards that adapt the lesson, timed exams with a weak-topic report, and a sketch board where you draw your answer.
MissionHand it a big goal and it plans the steps, then runs them one by one or all at once.
ComputerA Python computer inside the chat: it runs code, keeps files, and fixes its own errors.
PhonePrepares calendar events, Shortcuts, messages and maps for you to confirm with a tap.
StudioDescribe an app, a game or a simulation and it is built and running beside the chat, in a sandbox. Edit the code, run it again, switch between phone and desktop views.
DataDrop in a spreadsheet or CSV and get a dashboard: what stands out, charts to start with, and a table you can search.
MemoryA private list of facts, plans and reminders that stays on your device. Phraortes can remember and recall for you, and reminds you when something is due.
TasksHand off a bigger goal. It plans the steps, works through them while you do other things and brings back one answer. Choose quick, standard or deep.
PlansPlans are now Free, Plus, Pro and Max, with a new plan sheet and a faster checkout.
WebsiteA new home page and a refreshed look across every page.
AuraA full redesign: a living orb, five personalities (Nova, Atlas, Sage, Spark, Coach), five conversation modes, live word-by-word captions, tap-to-interrupt, a call timer and a summary with one-tap flashcards.
MenusLong-press a message for a new glass-style menu, in the iOS style.
EditEditing a question now replaces it and its answer in place. Deleting a question removes its answer too.
ChartsA new combined chart card: bars, lines and percentage rings together.
LearningPhraortes now learns how you study and adapts its explanations.
AnswersSmart cards for prices and weather: live coin and gold prices with a 24-hour chart, and an animated weather card.
SearchQuestions that need fresh facts are now searched before the model answers, with a built-in fallback when search is down.
LookA new compact sources button with real site icons, and a depth effect: the app steps back behind every panel.
CompanionThe golden bird now lives on the welcome screen and reacts to what you do.
