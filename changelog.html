Changelog

What is new.

Newest first. We ship small and often.

Newest

StudioDescribe an app, a game or a simulation and it is built and running beside the chat, in a sandbox. Edit the code, run it again, switch between phone and desktop views.
DataDrop in a spreadsheet or CSV and get a dashboard: what stands out, charts to start with, and a table you can search.
MemoryA private list of facts, plans and reminders that stays on your device. Phraortes can remember and recall for you, and reminds you when something is due.
TasksHand off a bigger goal. It plans the steps, works through them while you do other things and brings back one answer. Choose quick, standard or deep.
PlansPlans are now Free, Plus, Pro and Max, with a new plan sheet and a faster checkout.
WebsiteA new home page and a refreshed look across every page.
AuraA full redesign: a living orb, five personalities (Nova, Atlas, Sage, Spark, Coach), five conversation modes, live word-by-word captions, tap-to-interrupt, a call timer and a summary with one-tap flashcards.
MenusLong-press a message for a new glass-style menu, in the iOS style.
EditEditing a question now replaces it and its answer in place. Deleting a question removes its answer too.
ChartsA new combined chart card: bars, lines and percentage rings together.
LearningPhraortes now learns how you study and adapts its explanations.
AnswersSmart cards for prices and weather: live coin and gold prices with a 24-hour chart, and an animated weather card.
SearchQuestions that need fresh facts are now searched before the model answers, with a built-in fallback when search is down.
LookA new compact sources button with real site icons, and a depth effect: the app steps back behind every panel.
CompanionThe golden bird now lives on the welcome screen and reacts to what you do.

This week

ResearchDeep Research: parallel web searches, pages read, one sourced report. Quick or Deep.
SourcesA new sources panel: searches, found and read pages, excerpts, filters and copy-all links. Clear messages when a search fails.
CoresA new core picker with animated emblems, speed, depth and craft meters, and clear plan locks.
ShortcutsSiri and Shortcuts links open Phraortes with your question ready, or send it for you.
Free planThe 5-hour window now starts at your first message, not after the tenth.
FixThe companion bird now shows both eyes. The help panel is readable on the dark theme.

Latest

LookA living aurora behind the chat, a thinking orb that changes shape as it searches, builds or writes, and a new 3D welcome screen.
SiteA new dark-gold look, plus our story and this changelog.
ToolsLogic gates, an 8-bit CPU you can step through, and a breadboard with live current and resistor colour bands.
VoiceA smarter read-aloud player that picks the best voice on your device and reads maths naturally.
ChatImage attachments shrink to small swipeable thumbnails. A cleaner, faster Tools menu.
LookAccent colours, a repaired light theme and smart follow-up suggestions.
