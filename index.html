Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has all the study tools and the Prime core, with a limited number of messages per period.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser, on your device. They are not kept on our servers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in the browser and installs like an app if you want it on your home screen.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Persian, in text and in Aura voice.

Can the AI really operate the app?

Yes. It can open and close panels, switch chats, change the theme and the core, and more. Anything destructive asks you first.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

Payment is in USDT on the BEP-20 network. Pick a plan in the app and follow the steps.
