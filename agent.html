Hands-free

Just say “Hey Phraortes.”

Tap the Hey Phraortes chip once. Then keep your hands where they are: say the name, hear the chime, say what you want. It answers out loud and goes back to waiting.

  • Say the wake word. “Hey Phraortes” or «هی فراورتس», and it forgives mishearings.
  • Say it all at once. “Hey Phraortes, set a timer for ten minutes” works in one breath.
  • It speaks back. Quick actions get a short confirmation, questions get the answer read aloud.
  • Siri to start it. Add a Shortcut that opens Phraortes in listening mode, so “Hey Siri, Phraortes” starts the session from the lock screen.
What your iPhone allows

Honest about the limits

iOS decides when a web app may use the microphone, so we built around it instead of pretending.

  • Listening works while Phraortes is open and the screen is on. Not in the background, not on a locked screen.
  • The first start needs one tap each time the page loads. After that it is voice only.
  • Opening another app or placing a call needs one tap from you: Phraortes prepares it and shows the button.

Good to know

Questions.

Does it listen all the time?

Only after you tap the Hey Phraortes chip, and only while the app is open on screen. Turn it off with another tap. Nothing is recorded or kept on a server for the wake word.

Why do I have to tap once?

Safari does not let a web page start the microphone without a touch. That tap is the whole price of hands-free.

Does it work on the lock screen?

No. iOS does not allow background listening for web apps. Use a Siri Shortcut to open Phraortes in listening mode instead.

Will the alarm ring if I close the app?

Use Add to Calendar once per reminder. The built-in alert rings while the app is open, and Calendar rings in the background.

Is the agent free?

Yes. Every plan includes the agent, timers, the whiteboard and hands-free mode.
