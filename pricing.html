Billing

Paying, explained.

How do I pay?

Payment is in USDT on the BEP-20 network. Pick a plan in the app, send the payment, and it confirms automatically.

Do I need a card to start?

No. The free plan needs no card and no install.

Are the prices monthly?

Yes. The prices above are per month.

What happens when I hit a limit?

That core pauses until midnight. You can switch to another core, or move up a plan for higher limits.

Can the plans change?

They may. Plans and limits can change over time, so the app always shows the current details.
